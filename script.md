## Timing and accuracy notes — not spoken

- Aim to finish the spoken script at 4:45. The remaining 15 seconds are a buffer, not an extra feature section. Time a rehearsal; the target is not a guarantee of the recording duration.
- Speak while scrolling. Do not wait silently at the top and then explain the same content again below. Keep each repeated tab to about 4–6 seconds.
- Short pages such as Saved Areas need no artificial scrolling. For long permit pages, jump past repeated records to the note field rather than reading every permit. Keep the search and selection visible the first time.
- If over time, shorten transitions, remove repeated cursor movements, and trim the AI wait with a caption. Do not add another chatbot example or an optional workflow.
- If AI fails, say: “The AI request failed, but we can still read the figures and sources directly.” Do not narrate an expected response as though it appeared.
- The saved area pair and watchlist are shared across roles in this browser. Reopening them in another workspace does not create new entries.
- The permit snapshot currently contains 25 records across 2 parcel groups. Permit counts are not home counts. The tool does not independently verify completion or occupancy.
- Map labels have known metric and boundary-vintage inconsistencies. Use the clearly labeled cards and charts for specific numerical statements; do not interpret the map colors as one verified measure.
- Current housing availability, verified citywide completions, and household origin-to-destination flows are not provided. Do not claim that the platform already covers every Track 2 topic.
- Confirm the team’s actual AI-tool disclosure. The proposed civic pilot is a next step, not an existing partnership.
